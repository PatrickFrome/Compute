## Findings and limits

1. **HIGH priority, comparative source observation:** L1's shared Node process permits candidate source to affect shared runtime objects and time measurement despite a regex-only source filter. The presence of this flaw in `90fa5633...` and an actual numeric outcome manipulation are **NOT VERIFIED**; no candidate execution was attempted. Corrective direction: trusted measurement controller runs outside the candidate's process/isolate, sealed RPC protocol and trusted monotonic timing, with candidate outputs treated as untrusted data. A broader regex blacklist is **not** a sufficient fix.
2. **MEDIUM priority, provenance gap to trace:** mesh, tournament and experiment factories accept metrics/booleans and label them external, with digest verification. Verify where signed result/event producer identity enters the real consumer chain and ensure it cannot be replaced by caller-authored receipt claims. The promotion gate is separate; no implication that it is already circumvented.
3. **MEDIUM priority, paired-baseline scope:** L1 alone establishes no parent/control run. If used to claim *improvement*, a matched parent comparison is missing at that layer. A separate paired experiment ledger exists, but its linkage to L1 and target runtime is not verified.
4. **MEDIUM priority, data reuse:** benchmark provenance guard contains contamination and skill-memory fences. Persistent exposure/feedback identities across evaluator generations and the source of externally asserted task provenance require end-to-end verification in the exact target tree. No reuse vulnerability was proven.
5. **BLOCKER:** reconstructed c265 archive SHA-256 and post-patch tree SHA cannot be confirmed in this executor. No source patch to an unverified base, no claim of production qualification.
