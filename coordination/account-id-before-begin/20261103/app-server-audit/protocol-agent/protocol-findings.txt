inference_limits: Inference from these source facts: a nonempty usage accountId can corroborate the workspace of that usage snapshot against a separately obtained expected workspace ID on the trusted app-server connection. It cannot establish a unique human user, prove credential custody, identify a METAENGINE owner/registration/operation, or authorize later BEGIN/admission by itself. No returned auth epoch or cryptographic binding to a future job exists in these response types. Reconnects, account changes and stale results need client invalidation and a separately enforced execution-time identity/custody check.
